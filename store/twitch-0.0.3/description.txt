Everyone who writes in the stream chat gets a pixel hero, a level and XP. HOW TO USE: click the "C&A" sign button at the right edge of the video. A panel opens: tab "Профиль" shows your hero, level, XP bar, rank, duel wins and the points your level is worth (press "Показать мой профиль" and allow Twitch to share your ID); "Топ" shows the best players, click anyone to open their profile; "События" shows all channel awards and achievements. When a boss fight starts, a banner with the boss and its health bar appears at the top of the panel. Button "Фон" turns on a scene with all heroes currently in chat under the video; click a hero to see who it is. Subscribers, VIPs and mods get an XP bonus. STREAM OFFLINE? Press "Демо" in the panel: invented viewers run around. Demo runs locally, sends nothing (the button is hidden while the channel is live). No ads, no purchases, no payments.
